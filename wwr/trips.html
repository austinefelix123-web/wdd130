Our Featured Trips

Los Dientes del Diablo (Full Day)

An intense, high-adrenaline run through challenging Class IV rapids. Perfect for thrill-seekers and experienced rafters looking for an unforgettable challenge.

Big Mallard Expedition (Half Day)

A balanced mix of exciting rapids and calm floating sections. Ideal for families and intermediate adventures looking to experience the best of the river.

Scenic Family Float (Beginner)

Gentle Class I-II waters with plenty of wildlife viewing, swimming spots, and expert storytelling by our friendly guides.
